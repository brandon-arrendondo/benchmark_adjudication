# Unruled guidelines and open preset forms

As of 2026-10-10: none.

Every shipped guideline has a ruling. Where a rule's strict form was
ruled and its default or pedantic form was left open, that form is the
same as strict (`P/open-cells`). A guideline added later, or a preset
form a later ruling reopens, is listed here until it is ruled; a label
for a guideline listed here cites the principles only.
